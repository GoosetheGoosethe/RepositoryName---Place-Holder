        3. Weary- while any player is within a radius of 15 blocks of you, and you are not full health, gain 20 AE a second. At 300 AE, all players around you are exposed. At 600 AE, lose all AE and gain speed.
        4. Metal Heart- while afflicted with slowness, weakeness, or hunger, gain 20 AE a second for each of the listed status affects you are afflicted with. once you have no status affects, sneaking will give you slowness and regeneration, with a time equal to your AE/10.
        5. Ticker- while in a 4 block range of TNT or Fireballs, gain 20 AE a second. at 30 AE, spawn TNT on all players within 25 blocks of you, with a fuse of 2 seconds. additionally, gain 1 inert tnt.

        7. Hustle!- while slowed or sped, gain 20 AE a second, once you have 120 AE, gain speed and jump boost for 5 seconds, and reset your AE. additionally, you cannot be weakened.
        8. Rift Walker- while sneaking, gain 20 AE a second. once you have 80 AE, consume all AE and spawn an a rift that is only visble to you. after 10 seconds, teleport to your rift. spawning a rift will put you in a debt, of 220 AE. after teleporting, become exhausted.
        9. Libation- while sneaking, gain 20 AE a second. once you have 30 AE, become inflicted with fatal injury, afterwards, gain regeneration for 20 seconds, saturation II for 1 second, speed for 10 seconds, and absorbtion II for 30 seconds.

        11. Unremarkable- while there are no player within 8 blocks of you, gain 20 AE a second. once you reach 1k AE max your saturation and health.



Dictionary:
 -exhausted : an effect that causes you to be slowed, weakened, and hungry for 4 seconds. If you have full hunger while exhausted, you are not slowed or weakened.
 -exposed : an effect that causes you to glow, and have speed and strength strength removed for 4 seconds. If a player is within 3 blocks of you while exposed, gain slowness and stop glowing.
 -bleeding : an effect that causes you to take 1 heart of damage every 2 seconds for 8 seconds. players inflicted with bleeding cannot gain regeneration or absorbtion.
 -concused : an effect that causes you to be blind and unable to use items for 6 seconds.
 -revealed : an effect that causes the inflicting player to see a pillar of particals above you and below you.
 -fatal injury : players afflicted with fatal injury gain bleeding, poison, weakeness, and slowness for 8 seconds.
 -in air : a condition: if a player has air for 2.5 blocks beneath them
 -inert : an inert item has none of the special propeties it normally would.
